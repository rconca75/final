The Onion's American Voices

by Max McCalla, Rachel Conca, Elias Montas, and Rohit Tallapragada

About Our Project:

The Onion's "American Voices" series features satirical quotes from fake individuals and their thoughts on real events. For this series, they often reuse different images and attribute different names, jobs, and quotes to them.
Our project seeks to explore some of the data behind the reuse of these images and unearth trends within the use of photos
