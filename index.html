The Onion's American Voices

by Max McCalla, Rachel Conca, Elias Montas, and Rohit Tallapragada

About Our Project:

The Onion's American voices is a series that features fake on-the-street interviews with people about real-life events.

The series will feature photos of people along with a fake name, quote, and occupation. Some of the photos are consistently recycled in the series, with some appearing up to 4,000 times across different articles, while others only appear once or twice.

We wanted to visualize trends in photo apperances with an easy to use and understand interactive visualization. Enjoy!

How it Works

Photo Bar

Tool over the photos to see the photo's ID, how many times each photo was featured in the series, and what the photos it commonly appeared alongside. Photos it commonly appeared with will be lightly highlighted as well.

Click on a photo to see its appearnce trends over the years and hover over each point to see how many times the photo appeared that year.

Bar Graph

The bar graph shows how many quotes were published each year.

Tooling over the bars will show you the specific number of quotes for that year and highlight the bar you're seeing data from.
